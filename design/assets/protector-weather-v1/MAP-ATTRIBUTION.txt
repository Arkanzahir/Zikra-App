Bundled derivative protector_indonesia.geojson clips country polygon boundaries
to longitude 94..142 degrees east and latitude -13..8 degrees, simplifies the
coastline by at most approximately 0.022 degrees, and rounds coordinates to four
decimal places for a small offline map. It includes Indonesia and neighboring
countries. The map is an overview and does not represent navigation detail.
Plate-carree projection preserves the supplied geographic positions. Earthquake
markers are drawn only from actual BMKG coordinates supplied at runtime.
