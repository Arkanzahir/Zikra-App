# Public region catalog

`regions-myquran-v3.json` is the public response from
`https://api.myquran.com/v3/sholat/kabkota/semua`, retrieved 28 October 2026.
The 517 entries include additional island-specific regions; this count does not
claim 517 official kabupaten/kota. IDs are provider identifiers, not credentials.

Only names with an unambiguous match are selected automatically. Ambiguous or
unknown names require the user's explicit region choice. No nearest-city guess
is made. Monthly tables are downloaded separately and remain in internal app
storage; no user's coordinates or personal data are sent to this provider.
